# Success criteria

Decided 2026-10-19. **Review date: the end of the 2026-27 Premier League season (late May 2027).** Don't change these criteria before the review.

**Goal:** personal utility. I and my friends use it on matchdays.

| # | Criterion | Measured by |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | At least 50 visitors a week on average during the run-in (March–May 2027) | Cloudflare Web Analytics |
| 2 | Running cost of $0/month (hard ceiling: $5/month) | Bills |
| 3 | At most 1 hour of maintenance a month during the season | Maintenance log below |
| 4 | The Scorecard beats the Benchmark on the 2026-27 Snapshots (lower Brier score) | Scorecard |

**Kill rule:** if two or more criteria miss at the review, freeze the project. Leave it running while it still costs $0, but stop adding features.

**Spend triggers:** buy a custom domain only once criterion 1 is met.
